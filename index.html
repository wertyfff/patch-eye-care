LET’S CLEAR THINGS UP.

When should I use eye patches?

In the morning, before makeup or in the evening — whenever it fits your routine. Follow the product instructions for how often to use them.

How long should I leave them on?

Follow the time stated on the packaging. Different formulas have different instructions. Do not leave patches on overnight unless the product instructions say you can.

Should I keep them in the fridge?

Follow the storage instructions on the packaging. Some patches can be chilled before use for a cooling feel. Do not freeze them.

Can I reuse them?

Single-use patches are intended for one application. Dispose of them afterwards. Do not put used patches back in the jar.
